# Vela Privacy Policy

Vela is designed as a local-first macOS network controller.

Configurations and settings are stored in Application Support. Subscription credentials
and configured Wi-Fi names are stored in macOS Keychain.

Vela may contact user-configured subscription/provider servers, Vela's signed App update
feed, Vela's signed Mihomo core catalog, and links you explicitly open.

Vela doesn't include remote usage analytics and doesn't automatically upload crash
reports. Location permission is requested only for a Scene that matches the current
Wi-Fi name; Vela doesn't scan nearby networks or read BSSID.

Support bundles are generated and saved only after your explicit action. Nothing is
uploaded automatically.

This template requires product and legal review before publication.
