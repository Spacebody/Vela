# Vela Support Policy

Supported: macOS 15+, Apple Silicon, official signed and notarized Vela builds, the current
Stable release, and Mihomo cores from Vela's signed catalog or the Factory Core.

Intel Macs, modified privileged components, arbitrary cores, and unsigned repackaged Apps
aren't supported.

Open Help, run Diagnostics or Guided Support, preview a redacted Support Bundle, and attach
it manually through the project's support channel when appropriate. Never post credentials
or private configurations publicly.

Beta support is best effort. This template makes no response-time guarantee.
