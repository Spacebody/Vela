# V1 support policy

Supported scope is the official Developer ID signed and notarized arm64 App on macOS 15
or later, the current Stable release, the Factory Core, and compatible unblocked entries
from the signed Vela Core Catalog. When an immutable previous Stable actually exists and
is retained, it receives documented upgrade and recovery guidance. The first V1 launch
must not claim a previous-Stable guarantee until that artifact evidence is present.

Intel, Universal binaries, modified Helpers, repackaged or unsigned Apps, arbitrary
external Cores, manual root-store changes, and forced writes over a newer schema are not
supported. Beta and RC support is best effort. No response-time SLA is promised until an
accountable support organization has approved and staffed one.

Users are never asked to publish subscription secrets, private configuration, complete
logs, or unredacted support bundles.
