# Vela V1 Go / No-Go minutes

Record candidate version/build/commit/tag and the exact DMG, Public Contract,
Architecture Freeze, Documentation, Privacy, SBOM, and RC Manifest SHA-256 values.

For each required gate record status, immutable evidence, and accountable owner:
Stop-Ship, contracts, migration, security audit, 72-hour soak, performance,
accessibility/privacy, installation/update/rollback matrix, artifact/provenance,
and support/incident.

Unknown or missing evidence is No-Go. Every approval binds the exact version, build,
source commit, and final DMG SHA-256. Record one decision and approvals from Release,
Security, Reliability, Product, Support, and Accessibility/Localization reviewers.
